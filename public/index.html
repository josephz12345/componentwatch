BEHIND THE RADAR

A scraper with a memory.

ComponentWatch monitors VIPOutlet for RAM, SSDs, and GPUs. The local Node.js collector rotates through public collection pages, normalizes individual variants, and stores price and availability changes in SQLite.

PUBLIC FEED→COLLECTOR→SQLITE→DASHBOARD

A five-second scheduler handles one queued page per tick. Full inventory coverage depends on pagination and collection count. It honors robots.txt, conditional requests, Retry-After, and exponential backoff.

Demo mode: synthetic products and price history replay in your browser or local server. Live mode: run npm run live locally to collect real observations. The hosted demo does not run a background scraper.

Watchlists and price targets persist. Alerts appear in the activity log; no email or external messages are sent.
